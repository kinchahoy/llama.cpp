## Test-suite notes

The broad CTest run passed the ordinary CPU and model-format tests that completed. Multi-GPU `test-llama-archs` failed through the machine's known RCCL `ncclGroupEnd()` path; the same test passed in 66.66 seconds with `HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0`. The parallel `test-thread-safety` run hit a HIP graph-capture interaction and its isolated GPU rerun did not complete promptly. These generic multi-context tests are not representative of the supported launch configuration, which explicitly uses `GGML_CUDA_ALLREDUCE=internal`.

## Flash-Next capacity

The local 76.32 GiB UD-IQ3_XXS file maps its 26.82 GiB PLE tensor from disk. Resident GPU model buffers total about 50.2 GiB. At batch 512 and context 4352, compute, KV, and recurrent buffers add about 1.2 GiB across both GPUs.

The 93.7 GB `UD-IQ4_XS` quant is the largest published 4-bit-class candidate likely to fit fully offloaded on two 32 GiB GPUs with lazy PLE, batch 512, and a small context. Its margin is narrow. The 111 GB `UD-Q4_K_XL` and conventional Q4 variants are too large for full GPU offload and would require CPU-offloaded layers. Validate `UD-IQ4_XS` with `-fit-target` before downloading or committing to a large context.
